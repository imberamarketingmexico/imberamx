ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Engineered for Cold Impact

Imbera partners with Red Bull to deliver high-reliability commercial refrigeration technology designed for retail, convenience, and foodservice environments.

Precision Cooling

Rapid thermal pull-down performance keeps 8.6oz cans chilled at optimal serving temperatures even under demanding retail traffic.

Eco-Friendly Efficiency

Powered by natural hydrocarbon refrigerants (R290 & R600a) and compliant with Energy Star 4.0 and DOE 17 standards.

Maximum Visibility

Uniform LED interior illumination paired with crystal-clear glass maximizes Red Bull product visibility and impulse sales.

Certified Reliability

UL NSF 7 food-safety listed and engineered with low-maintenance commercial components built for 24/7 operation.
