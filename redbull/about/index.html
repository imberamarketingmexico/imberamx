ABOUT IMBERA & RED BULL

More than eight decades of commercial refrigeration engineering supporting high-impact beverage merchandising worldwide.

GLOBAL COMMERCIAL REFRIGERATION LEADER

Founded in 1941, Imbera is a global leader in commercial refrigeration design, manufacturing, and lifecycle service. Every merchandiser is built to deliver reliable pull-down times, uniform internal temperatures, and maximum shelf visibility across demanding retail channels.

From convenience stores and supermarkets to on-premise venues, our engineering teams continuously refine thermal efficiency, eco-friendly hydrocarbon refrigerants, and low-maintenance components.
